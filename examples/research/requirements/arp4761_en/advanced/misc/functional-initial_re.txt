The second hazard record applies to the TotalWBLossUnanunciated failure mode, cross-referenced to AIR6110 page 36, describing unanunciated total wheel braking loss occurring during the Landing and Rejected Takeoff flight phases, classified as a Hazardous failure condition with a Remote qualitative probability objective, associated with crew detection of failure when the brakes are operated, use of spoilers and thrust reversers to the maximum extent possible, and potential runway overrun, with an empty comment field. The third hazard record applies to the PartialSymmetricalWBLossUnAnunciated failure mode, cross-referenced to AIR6110 page 36, describing unanunciated partial symmetrical wheel braking loss occurring during the Landing and Rejected Takeoff flight phases, classified as a Hazardous failure condition with a Remote qualitative probability objective, with a comment noting the condition is potentially catastrophic pending confirmation by analysis, associated with crew detection of failure when the brakes are used, use of available wheel braking, spoilers and thrust reversers to the maximum extent to decelerate the aircraft, elevated loaded brake wheel temperatures that may reach the point of wheel or fire failure, and runway overrun risk dependent on the number of lost brakes.
